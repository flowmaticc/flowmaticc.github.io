2. What the app does with your traffic

Bloqueador uses Android's VPN feature to create a local VPN that doesn't connect to any external server. Only DNS queries go through it (the domain names apps want to reach, e.g. ads.example.com).

  • If the domain is on a block list, the app answers that it doesn't exist and the connection never happens.
  • If not, the query is forwarded to your usual DNS server (your carrier's or the one you've set), exactly as without the app.
  • All other traffic (websites, messages, videos…) doesn't go through the app. The app can't see the content of what you do.

3. What data it handles and where it stays

None of this data leaves the device. The app includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and excludes its data from Android backups.

4. The app's internet connections

  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, api.github.com, github.com): downloading block lists and checking for new app versions.
  • adaway.org: only if you turn on the optional AdAway layer.
  • The website you choose, only when you use “View on the web” in the history. It loads in a separate window, without your browser's cookies, and its data is deleted when you close it.

No personal data is sent in these downloads, but, as with any internet connection, those servers can see your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

5. Permissions

The complete list of permissions and what each is used for is on the Bloqueador page. The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, files, accessibility or screen capture.

6. Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. The block lists are the work of their authors (Hagezi, GoodbyeAds, 1Hosts, AdAway, StevenBlack) and are downloaded directly from their public repositories.

7. Your rights

Since we don't collect personal data, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. Local data (history and settings) is under your control: you can delete it from the app or by uninstalling it. For any question, write to gestiona.optimiza@gmail.com. You can also contact the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. License and warranty

Bloqueador is free software under the GNU GPL v3. It is provided without any warranty: blocking domains may stop some site or app from working properly. If so, you can allow the domain from the history or turn the filter off.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Code changes can be reviewed publicly on GitHub.
